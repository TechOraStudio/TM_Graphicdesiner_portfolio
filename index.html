Hi, I’m Tooba Murtaza, a passionate Graphic Designer and Video Editor dedicated to crafting bold, memorable visual identities and engaging dynamic stories.

I specialize in social media design, brand identity, logo systems, packaging, custom typography, vector illustration, and modern video editing.

As a designer, I focus on aesthetics that communicate with clarity and commercial edge. As a video editor, I transform raw footage into captivating motion through rhythm, smooth transitions, kinetic graphics, and visual effects.
